# SmartFinance — Demo Account Setup Guide

> **When to do this:** The night before your presentation.
> **Time needed:** ~30 minutes.
> **Goal:** Every screen in your demo script looks realistic, professional, and shows the right numbers at the right moment.

6. Return to app and log in with the credentials above

> At this point Security Score = 60 (email verified only)

---

## STEP 2 — Enable 2FA and Upload Profile Photo

### Enable 2FA:
1. Go to **Settings → Security**
2. Tap **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**
3. Enter your password when prompted
4. A 6-digit code is sent to your email — enter it
5. 2FA is now active → **Security Score becomes 100**

### Upload Profile Photo:
1. Go to **Settings → Profile** (or tap your avatar on Dashboard)
2. Tap **Edit Photo** → choose a photo from gallery
3. Use any professional-looking photo (even a placeholder portrait)

> Now when you show Section 2A, you can show Security Score = 100 and explain all three levels.
